Module 01 Applied Lab: Exploring the Rise of Agentic AI

Assignment Objective: Explore and evaluate the emergence of Agentic AI and its impact on modern workplace workflows through hands-on interaction with frontier AI tools.

Laboratory Instructions & Tasks

Step 1: Initial Discovery with Google Gemini

Navigate to Google Gemini (https://gemini.google.com). Select a real-world enterprise domain (e.g., project management, automated logistics, or software testing). Prompt Gemini to generate a comprehensive briefing analyzing how autonomous agents are transforming workflows in this sector. Export the resulting findings to a document.

Step 2: Knowledge Extraction in NotebookLM

Navigate to Google NotebookLM (https://notebooklm.google). Create a new notebook and upload the briefing generated in Step 1. Prompt NotebookLM to extract 3-5 key findings regarding workflow changes, autonomous tool use, and emerging human-in-the-loop roles. Identify 2 specific points requiring deeper verification.

Step 3: Deep Research Verification

Using ChatGPT, Perplexity (https://www.perplexity.ai), or Gemini Advanced, execute a deep research query on the points flagged in Step 2. Gather authoritative citations, real-world corporate case studies, and documented failure modes differentiating agentic systems from traditional workflow scripts.

Step 4: Synthesis Report

Write a 2-3 page executive report structured into: (1) Introduction & Domain Overview, (2) Key Workflow Transformations, (3) Critical Reflection on Tool Autonomy vs. Human Oversight, and (4) Verified Resource & Citation List with screenshots.
