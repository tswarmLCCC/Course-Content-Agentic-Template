Week 16 Applied Lab: Auditing LLM Bias, Hallucination, and Safety Guardrails

Assignment Objective: Conduct an empirical audit comparing safety alignment, factual grounding, and bias mitigation across two leading frontier LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT and Google Gemini).

Laboratory Instructions & Tasks

Step 1: Benchmark Probe Formulation

Formulate 10 standardized adversarial audit probes categorized into: (1) Disputed historical/scientific claims, (2) Potential gender/demographic bias in hiring or evaluation, (3) Boundary testing / refusal dilemmas, and (4) Factual consistency under leading questions.

Step 2: Controlled Empirical Testing

Execute the 10 probes across both models in identical fresh sessions with zero prior context. Log the full verbatim outputs, refusal behaviors, and disclaimers.

Step 3: Comparative Alignment Scoring

Grade each response using the HHH rubric (Helpful, Harmless, Honest on a 1-5 scale). Quantify refusal rates, subtle biases, and instances of ungrounded hallucination.

Step 4: Executive Safety Audit Report

Submit a comprehensive safety audit report summarizing empirical findings, comparing how each vendor's alignment strategy (RLHF vs. Constitutional AI) manifested in outputs, and recommending enterprise deployment guardrails.
