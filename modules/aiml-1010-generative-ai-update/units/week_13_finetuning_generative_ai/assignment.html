Week 13 Applied Lab: Curating Instruction Datasets & PEFT Fine-Tuning Strategy

Assignment Objective: Design, curate, and validate a standardized Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) instruction dataset formatted for Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA/QLoRA) model training.

Laboratory Instructions & Tasks

Step 1: Domain & Syllabus Corpus Selection

Select two chapters or technical topics from a computer science or data engineering textbook. Identify 10 core competencies, definitions, and problem-solving patterns.

Step 2: Instruction Dataset Curation (Alpaca/ShareGPT Format)

Author a structured dataset of 50-100 high-quality instruction-response pairs. Each entry must strictly adhere to the standardized schema: `{"instruction": "...", "input": "...", "output": "..."}`.

Step 3: Quality Guidelines & Anti-Hallucination Annotation

Write an annotation guideline rubric specifying: (1) Tone and conciseness rules, (2) Formatting standards (code blocks, bulleting), and (3) Negative constraint handling (how the model should respond when context is insufficient).

Step 4: LoRA Hyperparameter Proposal & Submission

Develop a proposed LoRA/QLoRA fine-tuning training configuration (target modules e.g., `q_proj, v_proj`, rank r=16, alpha=32, learning rate 2e-4, batch size). Submit your finalized `.json`/`.csv` dataset file along with your annotation guideline document.
