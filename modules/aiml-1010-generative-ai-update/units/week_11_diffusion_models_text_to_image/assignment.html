Week 11 Applied Lab: Cross-Modal Generative AI & Image Synthesis

Assignment Objective: Investigate and compare the mathematical and operational mechanics of generative diffusion models across image synthesis, inpainting, and cross-modality transformations.

Laboratory Instructions & Tasks

Step 1: Modality Selection

Select two distinct generative modality processes: (A) Text-to-Image Synthesis vs. (B) Inpainting / Outpainting image reconstruction.

Step 2: Empirical Parameter Investigation

Using an interactive environment (e.g., Hugging Face Spaces, Automatic1111, or OpenVINO notebooks), run generation experiments systematically varying: (1) Sampling Steps (10 vs 30 vs 50), (2) Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG scale 3.0 vs 7.5 vs 15.0), and (3) Random Seeds.

Step 3: Comparative Analysis & Inpainting Evaluation

Analyze the trade-offs: how does step count affect high-frequency image details vs. latency? In the inpainting experiment, evaluate how seamless the latent boundary blends with masked image regions.

Step 4: Formal Research Report

Produce a formal comparative laboratory report with annotated visual comparisons, parameter grids, discussion of VAE latent compression, and ethical considerations surrounding synthetic imagery.
