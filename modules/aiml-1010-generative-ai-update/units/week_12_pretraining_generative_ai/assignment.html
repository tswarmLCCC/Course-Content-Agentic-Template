Week 12 Applied Lab: Domain-Specific LLM Architecture & Pre-Training Case Study

Assignment Objective: Conduct an in-depth comparative study analyzing pre-training corpora, tokenization strategies, and domain adaptation in specialized industrial foundation models.

Laboratory Instructions & Tasks

Select four specialized open-weights domain models from Hugging Face representing distinct verticals (e.g., Code: StarCoder/CodeLlama; Biomedicine: BioMedLM/BioMistral; Finance: FinGPT/BloombergGPT; Mathematics: Llama-Math).

Step 4: Comparative Research Paper
