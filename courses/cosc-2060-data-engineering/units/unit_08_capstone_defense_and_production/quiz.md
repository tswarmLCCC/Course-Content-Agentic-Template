| **3** | **B** | When datasets exceed available RAM, in-memory engines spill intermediate hash tables and partitions to disk, shifting throughput from RAM to disk I/O. |

| **7** | **B** | Professional engineers embrace edge cases: identifying blast radius, failure containment, and mitigation demonstrates senior competence. |

| **9** | **B** | Blast radius measures failure containment: defensive design isolates components so a crashed ingestor does not take down analytical queries. |
