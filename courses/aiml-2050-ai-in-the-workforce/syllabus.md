## 1. Course Description

An accelerated, applied examination of agentic artificial intelligence and its integration into modern enterprise workforce environments. Designed to prepare students for high-demand workplace competencies, this course spans strategic opportunity assessment, ethical risk governance, rapid prototyping, no-code workflow orchestration, Python tool calling with LangChain/LangGraph, automated system evaluation, and sovereign local AI deployment on Intel PC hardware. Emphasizes hands-on workplace projects, practical toolchains, and executive defense of AI initiatives.

---

## 2. Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)

Upon successful completion of AIML 2050, students will be able to:
1. **CLO 1 (Opportunity Auditing)**: Analyze enterprise business workflows to quantify ROI, labor impact, and feasibility for agentic AI automation.
2. **CLO 2 (Governance & Safety)**: Formulate enterprise governance charters applying NIST AI RMF and human-in-the-loop escalation gates to autonomous systems.
3. **CLO 3 (Rapid Prototyping)**: Rapidly prototype conversational and task-oriented agents using modern workplace platforms connected to live data sources.
4. **CLO 4 (No-Code Orchestration)**: Architect multi-step workflows (n8n/Relay/Zapier) that combine LLM reasoning with deterministic APIs and automated error recovery.
5. **CLO 5 (Tool Calling & Schemas)**: Implement programmatic tool-calling agents in Python using robust Pydantic schemas, HTTP/API data contracts, and automated validation tests.
6. **CLO 6 (Stateful Multi-Agent Orchestration)**: Build persistent, stateful multi-turn agents using LangGraph and SQLite checkpoints with supervisor approval gates.
7. **CLO 7 (System Evaluation & Observability)**: Instrument agent execution telemetry with LangSmith to evaluate accuracy, drift, latency, and prompt injection resilience.
8. **CLO 8 (Sovereign Local AI)**: Deploy and benchmark sovereign local AI models and agents on Intel PC hardware (Ollama, OpenVINO, SQLite, FAISS) ensuring zero cloud data leakage.
9. **CLO 9 (Capstone Defense)**: Defend an end-to-end workforce AI implementation in an executive capstone presentation demonstrating commercial viability, architecture, and risk controls.

## 4. Weekly Pedagogical Sequence (The 6-Hour Contact Hour Budget)

Every weekly module is scaffolded progressively to ensure student success on the applied project:
1. **Unit Overview & Roadmap (30m)**: Clear problem statement, workforce context, contact hour breakdown.
2. **Why — Required Readings & Concepts (90m)**: Conceptual framework, real-world workforce use cases, visual architecture diagrams.
3. **What — Interactive Drills (60m)**: Formative checks with `<details>` accordions and Socratic AI roleplay practice.
4. **How — Guided Lab Walkthrough (60m)**: Step-by-step technical implementation with error handling.
5. **Produce — Applied Weekly Project (90m)**: Independent, workplace-grade deliverable graded via native Canvas rubric.
6. **Verify — Knowledge Check (30m)**: 15-question native QTI 1.2 assessment.
