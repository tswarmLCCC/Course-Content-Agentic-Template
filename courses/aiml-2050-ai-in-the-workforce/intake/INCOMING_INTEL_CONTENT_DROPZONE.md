# Intel Curriculum Intake Dropzone: AIML 2050 (AI in the Workforce)

Welcome to the intake staging area for **AIML 2050: AI in the Workforce**.

This folder is designed to receive all raw, unstructured, or semi-structured curriculum materials provided from Intel (or other workforce partner sources) before we formalize the 8-week course plan.

---

## 1. Supported Drop Formats

You can drop files directly into this `intake/` folder or paste content in any format:
- **Table of Contents / Outlines**: Text, Markdown, or docx outlines of the source units.
- **Presentations & Slide Decks**: PowerPoint (`.pptx`), PDF (`.pdf`), or Google Slides exports.
- **Lecture Notes & Transcripts**: Markdown files, text dumps, or video transcripts.
- **Lab Exercises / Notebooks**: Jupyter Notebooks (`.ipynb`), Python scripts (`.py`), or lab guide documents.
- **Direct Chat Input**: Simply paste the Table of Contents or list of units in our chat conversation.
