## Slide 2
- Introduction to Agentic AI
- 2

| Course Description | Introduction to Agentic AI systems and their emerging role in intelligent software and digital workflows. Learners will examine the societal implications of Agentic AI, apply agentic tools for workplace productivity, prototype agent-powered solutions, and progressively build AI agents using both no-code platforms and coding frameworks. The course culminates in deploying local AI agents with attention to safety, governance, and real-world operational constraints. |
| Learning Objectives | Define Agentic AI and differentiate it from automation, traditional AI, and generative AI systems. Identify key components of AI agents including reasoning loops, tool usage, memory, and workflow orchestration.  Evaluate the impact of Agentic AI on organizations, productivity, and emerging roles in the workforce. Prototype practical applications using modern agentic AI tools and workflow automation platforms. Build and deploy basic AI agents using both no-code platforms and coding frameworks. Apply responsible AI principles including governance, safety, and human oversight when deploying AI agents. |
