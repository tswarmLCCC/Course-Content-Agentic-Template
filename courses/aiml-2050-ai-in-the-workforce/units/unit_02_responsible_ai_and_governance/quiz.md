### Question 1
Which of the following is the most important consideration in the ethical use of AI?
* A) Maximizing profit for organizations
* B) Ensuring fairness, transparency, and respect for human rights
* C) Deploying AI as quickly as possible
* D) Reducing the number of human employees

### Question 2

* A) AI systems will always make perfect decisions
* B) Reduction in data storage needs
* C) Increased computational speed
* D) Amplification of bias and discrimination

### Question 3

* A) Using AI to manipulate voter behavior through targeted misinformation
* B) Creating AI systems that prioritize profit over user privacy
* C) Deploying AI for early disease detection with patient consent
* D) Implementing social credit scoring without transparency

### Question 4

* A) Accountability
* B) Explainability
* C) Privacy
* D) Autonomy

* A) Humans should have the ability to review and override AI decisions
* B) AI systems should operate without any monitoring
* C) AI should replace all human decision-making

* B) The AI achieves its objectives faster than expected
* C) The AI system costs more than anticipated

* B) A hospital AI autonomously cancels surgeries without consulting staff
* C) An AI requires human approval for every decision

* B) A software tool for building AI models
